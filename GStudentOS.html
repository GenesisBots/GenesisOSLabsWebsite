GStudentOS is Customizable

GStudentOS is a full student learning operating system — built with Agents to help you understand your material, organize your semester, & study smarter. And because your data is local-first, your academic life stays private & under your control.

  • Personalized exam practice using your local notes, progress, and study history stay on your devices.

  • Create quizzes, study guides, and flash cards based on your exact course materials.
