Before you book

Questions operators ask

What is Aerys Desktop?

Aerys Desktop is a Company Operating System from GenesisOS Labs. It gives AI agents, workflows, data, and operations one shared context, so the business is not run through disconnected software.

Do we have to replace our entire stack at once?

No. Engagements start with the workflows you choose and expand department by department.

Can Aerys run on our own hardware?

Yes. Aerys can run local-first, in the cloud, or as a hybrid. You choose what stays on hardware you control.

Who is the strategy demo for?

Founders, operators, and functional leaders who need one operating environment and do not want to staff a full engineering team to get there.

Optional, and later

Advanced infrastructure stays out of the way

Aerys works as your Company OS without anything else. Some organizations later extend the same environment with additional GenesisOS Labs infrastructure, including GCoin. It stays optional and outside daily operations until you ask for it.
