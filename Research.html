How GResearch works

GResearch carries a scientific record from intake to command. Knowledge stays attached to its evidence, its people, and the decision it supports.

  1. 01

    Acquire

    Collect the scientific record, monitor sources, and ingest knowledge into the platform.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Keep scientific memory, read relationships, and surface candidates and research gaps.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Test evidence integrity and trace provenance from a result back to its source.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    Resolve authors, institutions, funding, and canonical research identities.

  5. 05

    Operate

    Direct the work from the executive dashboard, Mission Control, and the command center.

GenesisOS Labs built GResearch so a research program could acquire knowledge, preserve the evidence, and direct the work from one command center.

GResearch v0.19.0

The GResearch system

Acquisition, memory, discovery, integrity, identity, and command stay in one operating system. The record remains connected to the authors, institutions, funding, and evidence that produced it.

Acquire

  • Scientific Acquisition Bring papers, records, and source material into the system.
  • Research Collection Hold the working collection as one research body.
  • Source Monitoring Watch sources for what enters or changes the record.
  • Knowledge Ingestion Turn incoming material into structured knowledge.

Understand

  • Scientific Memory Retain the record so later work can use what was already established.
  • Knowledge Intelligence Read the body of knowledge for relationships and meaning.
  • Discovery Platform Move from what is known toward what is worth pursuing.
  • Research Candidates Surface candidate findings and lines of inquiry.
  • Research Gap Intelligence Identify what the record does not yet cover.

Trust

  • Evidence Integrity Test whether the evidence still supports the claim attached to it.
  • Funding Intelligence See how funding connects to authors, institutions, and lines of work.
  • Author Intelligence See who produced the work and how the contributions relate.
  • Identity Resolution Resolve people, institutions, and works to canonical identities.
  • Research Provenance Trace a result back through the evidence that produced it.

Operate

  • Executive Dashboard Give leadership one view of the research record.
  • Mission Control Direct active research from one operating surface.
  • Research Operations Run the day-to-day work of the research program.
  • Command Center Hold acquisition, trust, and action in one command center.
