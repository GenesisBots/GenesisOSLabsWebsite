By GenesisOS Labs

Lead your campus into the future of cryptography, tokenization, and digital asset engineering.

Program Overview

Student Ambassadors will bring cryptography, tokenization, and Genesis Coin use cases into real campus environments. You’ll help classmates learn through coding and hands-on building, represent GenesisOS Labs at your school, and create a pathway from classroom projects to real-world, production-grade digital asset development.

  • Learn then teach classmates how cryptography and tokenization show up in real products & use cases
  • Code & ship tokenized projects on digital assets like XRP, XLM, XDC, HBAR, Algorand, & our L2 GCoin.
  • Represent GenesisOS Labs on campus as the local operator for workshops, onboarding, and events.
  • Connect students to GStudentOS, GCoin, and the wider GenesisOS Labs community.

Benefits

Ambassadors get tools, training, and mentorship — and leave with work experience that belongs on a résumé.

Early access

Beta test GenesisOS Labs products like GGamingOS before they are broadly released, including campus workflows on GStudentOS and Genesis Coin.

Cryptography + tokenization training

Build fluency in cryptography protocols like ISO 20022, DLT, & tokenization so you can teach others with confidence.

Résumé-ready experience

Lead workshops, ship tokenized projects, and document outcomes you can show employers.

Direct mentorship

Work with GenesisOS Labs operators on real questions from campus builds and events.

Community + events

Join a growing network of ambassadors, builders, and campus events across schools.

Responsibilities

Ambassadors operate on campus. You host, teach, onboard, & represent the brand.

Host workshops

Run sessions on cryptography, tokenization, and digital asset engineering for classmates.

Help students build tokenized projects

Coach peers from idea to a working tokenized projects like video game NFTs on GCoin rails.

Support GStudentOS onboarding

Help students start GStudentOS and use it for notes, coursework, exams, and campus builds.

Represent GenesisOS Labs

Show up at campus events as the GenesisOS Labs Ambassador for your school.

Apply by email

There is no form. Email GenesisOS Labs your résumé and/or a link to your LinkedIn profile. We’ll select up to four student ambassadors per campus.
