Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

GenesisOS Labs LLC (“GenesisOS Labs”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit GenesisOSLabs.com or submit forms describing interest in The Genesis System or GenesisOS.

1. Overview

We collect only what we need to respond to inquiries, operate the website securely, and improve clarity for visitors.

2. Data We Collect

Contact details, message content you submit, referral information (if provided), and basic technical diagnostics such as browser type and approximate region derived from standard web logs.

3. Data Use

We use collected information to respond to requests, provide services, prevent abuse, and maintain platform security. We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Forms may be processed by trusted providers (for example Formspree) subject to their terms. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. Some pages load X (Twitter) embed widgets to display public posts. Review those providers’ policies if you need transfer details.

5. Retention

We retain inquiry data long enough to complete follow-up and satisfy legal obligations, unless a longer period is required by contract or law.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email support@GenesisOSLabs.com.
