Custom LMS for Your University or Business

By GenesisOS Labs

A fully agentic, auditable, customizable learning management system that runs inside Aerys Desktop or web browser — built specifically for your institution.

Our LMS is not a one‑size‑fits‑all platform. It is a dedicated module inside a customized Aerys Desktop build, capable of running agentic operations for faculty, staff, and students. Every workflow, screen, and module is fully customizable — no vendor lock‑in, no rigid templates, no hidden constraints.

Why a Custom LMS

  • Your Enterprise or Institution owns the code, the workflows, and the future roadmap.
  • Every feature can be tailored to faculty, staff, and student needs.
  • Runs locally-first or in the cloud, depending on your deployment preference.
  • Browser version available for Mac, PC, and standard web environments.
  • Optional skins/themes so students and faculty can choose the interface that fits their workflow.

Blockchain‑Backed Academic & Business Integrity (Optional)

  • Every grade, attendance record, approval, and academic artifact can be cryptographically validated.
  • Business & Institutional data becomes tamper‑proof — no silent overwrites, no corruption.
  • Each data point can be minted as an asset and verified by the Business or University LLM.
  • Minimizes hallucinations and prevents AI from fabricating or altering records.
